About this prototype

This is a research and educational prototype for predicting knee osteoarthritis progression (ΔKL ≥ 1 over a 48-month window) from baseline clinical features in the Osteoarthritis Initiative (OAI) cohort. It is not a certified diagnostic device and must never replace professional medical evaluation. The calculator on this page runs a simplified estimate directly in your browser for demonstration purposes.
